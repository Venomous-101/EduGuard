TITLE: UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers (2024)
SOURCE_URL: https://doi.org/10.54675/ZJTE2084

UNESCO's AI Competency Framework for Teachers (2024), developed by Fengchun Miao and Mutlu Cukurova, guides teachers in acquiring the competencies required in the age of AI and supports their adaptive professional development.

The framework defines 5 competency dimensions, each with 3 progression levels (Acquire, Deepen, Create):
1. Human-centred mindset: human agency, human accountability, AI social responsibility. Teachers progress from understanding human agency to ensuring human accountability to co-creating AI social responsibility.
2. Ethics of AI: ethical principles, safe and responsible use, co-creating AI ethics rules.
3. AI foundations and applications: basic AI techniques and applications, application skills, creating with AI.
4. AI pedagogy: AI-assisted teaching, AI-pedagogy integration, AI-enhanced pedagogical transformation.
5. AI for professional development: AI-enabled lifelong learning, AI-enhanced organizational learning, AI-supported professional transformation.

The framework addresses the teacher-student-AI triangular relationship: AI's ability to emulate human behavior raises concerns about undermining teachers' roles, so teachers must understand both benefits and limitations of AI, integrate AI tools into pedagogy effectively, and leverage them for professional development.

On safe use, the framework requires teachers to model safe and responsible AI use, understand data privacy implications of AI tools in the classroom, and ensure AI enhances rather than replaces pedagogical judgment. Teachers must be able to evaluate AI outputs critically and maintain accountability for learning outcomes.

TITLE: UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data - principles
SOURCE_URL: https://elaws.gov.ae/EnLegislations.aspx

Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 is the UAE's first federal data protection law. It establishes the principles governing the processing of personal data in the UAE.

Core principles relevant to AI use in schools:
- Lawful and fair processing: personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently.
- Purpose limitation: data collected for a specified, explicit purpose must not be processed for incompatible purposes (e.g., student data collected for learning must not be reused to train commercial AI models).
- Data minimization: only data necessary for the stated purpose may be processed.
- Consent: processing generally requires the consent of the data subject; for children, parental or guardian consent is required.
- Data subject rights: individuals have rights to access, correct, and erase their personal data.
- Security: controllers and processors must implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data.
- Cross-border transfers: transferring personal data outside the UAE is subject to conditions and safeguards.

For schools deploying AI tools: any AI system that processes student personal data (names, grades, biometric data, learning analytics) must have a lawful basis, documented consent (parental consent for minors), a data-processing agreement with the vendor, and guarantees that student data is not used for unrelated purposes such as model training.

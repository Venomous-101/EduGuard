TITLE: Abu Dhabi AI Governance (AIATC) and MBZUAI Education Role
SOURCE_URL: https://www.financemiddleeast.com/fintech/ai/uae-president-reconstitutes-artificial-intelligence-and-advanced-technology-council/

Abu Dhabi's artificial intelligence ecosystem is coordinated by the Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Technology Council (AIATC) and anchored academically by the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). Together, governance and education form the institutional backbone through which AI policy reaches classrooms and institutions in the emirate.

The AIATC was established in January 2024 by the UAE President, acting in his capacity as Ruler of Abu Dhabi. It is responsible for regulating, implementing, and developing policies and strategies related to artificial intelligence and advanced technologies across Abu Dhabi. Its mandate includes fostering research, investment, and collaboration with local and international partners, with the stated aim of positioning Abu Dhabi as a global leader in AI and advanced technology.

The Council is chaired by Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Deputy Ruler of Abu Dhabi, with Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan as Vice-Chairman. Its membership includes senior officials and business figures: Khaldoon Khalifa Al Mubarak, Ahmed Mubarak bin Nawi Al Mazrouei, Sultan Ahmed Al Jaber, Jassem Mohamed Bu Ataba Al Zaabi, Mohamed Hassan Al Suwaidi, Faisal Abdulaziz Al Bannai, and Peng Xiao. The UAE President reconstituted the Council, confirming its central role in the emirate's AI strategy alongside major initiatives such as the MGX investment fund for global AI investment.

MBZUAI, based in Abu Dhabi, is a graduate-level research university dedicated entirely to artificial intelligence and is recognized as one of the world's leading AI education institutions. It offers fully funded M.Sc. and Ph.D. programs in computer science, computer vision, machine learning, natural language processing, robotics, statistics and data science, and computational biology, with full tuition waivers, monthly stipends, health insurance, and residence visas. It also runs a self-funded Master in Applied AI for working professionals in the UAE.

In 2025, MBZUAI unveiled a first-of-its-kind undergraduate program in artificial intelligence, open to local and international students. The program offers two streams: a Bachelor of Science in AI for Business, focused on business integration and entrepreneurship, and a Bachelor of Science in AI for Engineering, focused on AI model development, deployment, and real-world applications. The curriculum is deliberately cross-disciplinary, combining humanities, business, and liberal arts with technical training, and includes cooperative industry placements, internships, and mentorships with industry and AI research partners. Students have access to high-end AI computing resources, smart classrooms, and incubation spaces.

The university's governance reflects its national importance. Its Board of Trustees, reconstituted in 2025 under the directives of the UAE President and by resolution of the AIATC Chairman, is chaired by Khaldoon Khalifa Al Mubarak and includes figures such as Jassem Al Zaabi, Rima Al Mokarrab Al Muhairi, Professor Daniela Rus, AMD CEO Dr. Lisa Su, and MBZUAI President Professor Eric Xing. In December 2025, the Tahnoon bin Zayed Scholarship in AI Excellence was established under the guidance of the AIATC Chairman to support outstanding undergraduate students at MBZUAI, reinforcing the leadership's commitment to developing AI talent.

For schools, MBZUAI's education role is both direct and indirect. It is a named partner of the UAE Ministry of Education's national AI school curriculum rollout alongside Presight, AI71, and the Emirates College for Advanced Education, contributing research and AI expertise to the K-12 initiative. Its graduates and research feed the specialist teacher pipeline and the broader AI workforce the school curriculum is meant to produce. Abu Dhabi's own education reforms, including the "Artificial Intelligence and Technology" subject in government schools and ADEK-led AI literacy initiatives for private schools, operate within the policy environment shaped by the AIATC.

In summary, Abu Dhabi governs AI through the AIATC's policy and regulatory mandate, builds AI talent through MBZUAI's undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs, and connects both to schools through partnerships with the national AI curriculum and emirate-level education regulators.

TITLE: UAE Charter for the Development and Use of AI (2024) and AI ethics principles
SOURCE_URL: https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/policy/details/the-uae-charter-for-the-development-and-use-of-artificial-intelligence

The UAE Charter for the Development and Use of AI (2024) sets out 12 priorities for responsible AI development and deployment in the UAE. It builds on the UAE's earlier AI ethics principles and guidelines.

The Charter's priorities cover: safety and security of AI systems, fairness and non-discrimination, transparency and explainability, accountability with clear human responsibility, privacy protection, and human-centric design ensuring AI serves human wellbeing.

Crucially, the Charter and the UAE's AI ethics frameworks are nonbinding: they articulate principles without enforcement mechanisms. Independent assessments note this limits their practical reach: organizations, including schools, face no binding obligation to comply and no penalties for non-compliance.

This creates the policy gap EduGuard UAE is designed to fill: between the UAE's high-ambition AI strategy (mandatory AI curriculum, significant AI investment) and the absence of an operational compliance layer for AI deployed in classrooms. An evidence-anchored compliance agent provides the missing enforcement mechanism: checking real classroom AI use cases against codified policy and flagging violations before deployment.

Dubai's AI ethics principles and guidelines (Digital Dubai) similarly provide nonbinding guidance for AI developers and deployers in the emirate, emphasizing fairness, transparency, accountability, and human oversight.
